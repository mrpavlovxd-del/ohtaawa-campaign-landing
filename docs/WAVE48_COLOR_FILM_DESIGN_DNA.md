# Wave48 color-film Design DNA

## Product truth

The visitor is not only protecting paint. They are choosing a new visual identity for the car while preserving the factory coating underneath. The page must balance emotional color appeal with credible installation discipline.

## Selected architecture: editorial dual perspective

1. Concrete hero: full color-film wrapping, fixed `230 000 RUB`, one consultation CTA.
2. Color as a deliberate choice: large controlled surface detail, no swatch inventory claims.
3. Real OHTAAWA case: paired front/rear views of the same wrapped vehicle.
4. What is included: preparation, material, installation and final control in compact editorial rows.
5. Process, duration, warranty and taxi.
6. Reviews, real map, FAQ and final CTA.

## Signature component

`Two perspectives, one finish`: a full-width code-native gallery pairing front and rear real photographs. The selected view occupies the stage; the second view remains visible as a narrow perspective rail. On mobile it becomes a simple two-step gallery with explicit controls and stable dimensions.

## Visual language

- Foundation: OHTAAWA near-black, cool ivory and restrained lime.
- Service accent: deep cool burgundy sampled from the real vehicle, used sparingly for rules, active state and metadata, not as a page-wide tint.
- Typography: existing Wave45 serif/sans family, but with shorter headline blocks and a more editorial rhythm.
- Photography: real color finish must be visible in neutral daylight and close crop; atmospheric hero may use controlled studio light but cannot replace proof.
- Motion: short crossfade and directional gallery movement only; no decorative parallax or perpetual animation.

## Must avoid

- car configurator or unverified color swatches;
- fake before/after continuity;
- generic luxury slogans, technical service labels or `premium` repeated as proof;
- floating cards, diagram-like infographics, gradient backgrounds and generated component boards;
- brown/orange dominance or a monochrome burgundy page;
- any statement that all panoramic glass, windshield or unpainted parts are included.

## Reference impact

- Wave45 contributes offer clarity, brand lockup, contact dialog and live map.
- Wave46 contributes shorter service-specific pacing and no fixed mobile CTA.
- Real OHTAAWA front/rear color-film images provide factual identity and location proof.
- The dual-perspective stage and burgundy material accent create visible divergence from the transparent-film pages.
