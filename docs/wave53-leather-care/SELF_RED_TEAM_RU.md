## What was challenged

1. Does the page prove a paid channel? No. It is a measurable baseline for a new cohort, not evidence of Search efficiency.
2. Does the real hero prove Koch application or an OHTAAWA case? No. Copy and provenance do not frame it that way; production use is blocked pending owner confirmation.
3. Is the absence of a gallery a weakness? It is a deliberate safety decision until truthful same-surface/process assets exist.
4. Does the page drift into full dry cleaning or restoration? No. The exclusions appear immediately after scope and again in condition/FAQ copy.
5. Is the first step abstract? No. «Узнать ближайшее время» and the prepared message name the service and price.
6. Is mobile a reduced desktop clone? No. Mobile uses a dedicated real crop, keeps price and CTA in the first viewport, collapses the care ledger without losing it and uses a single-column contact sheet.
