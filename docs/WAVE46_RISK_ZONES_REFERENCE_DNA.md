# Wave46 reference DNA

## Binding benchmark

- Approved Wave45 hero scale, OHTAAWA lockup, restrained black/ivory/lime palette, price hierarchy, contact dialog, real map, and proof carousel.
- Owner-approved mobile correction: no sticky CTA that covers content.

## What changes materially

- Shorter architecture focused on one front-zone decision.
- Hero composition emphasizes the nose, hood, bumper, and side mirror rather than the whole-body silhouette.
- Package scope appears before general brand storytelling.
- Proof is closer to the price and first CTA.
- No taxi promise because it applies to full-body wrapping, not this package.

## Must avoid

- generic luxury-car stock composition;
- oversized hands or implausible tools;
- illustrated zone maps assembled from basic shapes;
- claims that a generated scene is a completed customer job;
- repeated caveats that undermine the fixed-price promise;
- technical labels such as PPF in customer-facing headings.
