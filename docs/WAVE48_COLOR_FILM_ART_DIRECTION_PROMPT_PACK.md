# Wave48 Product Art Direction Prompt Pack

## Target purpose

Create one internal desktop visual target for a premium Russian automotive
service landing. The target must prove the selected `editorial dual
perspective` architecture and guide HTML/CSS implementation. It is not a public
advertisement, product screenshot or client-work photograph.

## Product truth to express

- Full-body color protective-film wrapping as both visual transformation and
  paint protection.
- Fixed `230 000 RUB` offer should have unmistakable hierarchy.
- Real local studio, controlled installation and a tangible color finish.
- Signature component: two perspectives of one deep-burgundy wrapped SUV.

## Composition anchors

1. Slim near-black header with authentic OHTAAWA lockup and one compact contact
   action.
2. Asymmetric first viewport: short editorial headline and fixed price on cool
   ivory; atmospheric color-film surface or vehicle crop on the opposite side.
3. Immediate `Two perspectives, one finish` stage: dominant front perspective
   and a narrow rear-view rail, with clean code-native control placeholders.
4. Compact editorial rows for included work, duration, warranty and taxi.
5. Restrained lower proof/contact rhythm; no repeated card grid.

## Material and type language

- Near-black ink, cool ivory paper, restrained OHTAAWA lime and a thin deep
  cool-burgundy material accent.
- High-contrast editorial serif for large headlines; neutral modern sans for
  service copy and controls.
- Large photographic planes, precise rules, generous but purposeful whitespace.
- Subtle film-edge or color-shift surface detail is permitted as atmosphere.

## Must include

- Clear fixed-price hierarchy.
- A visible front/rear dual-perspective signature stage.
- Stable, implementable desktop layout with real navigation and CTA regions.
- Natural automotive proportions and plausible reflections.
- A structure that can collapse cleanly to one mobile column.

## Must avoid

- Final readable long-form copy, fake logos, fake testimonials, fake plate text
  or invented technical specifications.
- Generic luxury slogans, gradients, glassmorphism, floating cards, diagrams,
  swatch configurators, nested panels or a generic SaaS layout.
- Mountain/forest scenery, dealership showroom cliches, toy-like cars,
  impossible body anatomy, hands or workers.
- Treating generated media as proof or recreating the real photographs inside a
  synthetic case study.

## Concise native execution prompt

High-fidelity desktop landing-page concept target, 1440x1000, for OHTAAWA, a
premium automotive detailing studio in Saint Petersburg. Editorial dual-
perspective architecture for full-body color protective-film wrapping at a
fixed 230,000-ruble offer. Near-black, cool ivory, restrained lime, thin deep
cool-burgundy accents. Slim branded header; asymmetric hero with short Russian
headline and large fixed price; cinematic close crop of a deep-burgundy wrapped
SUV under controlled studio light; immediately below, a signature real-photo
stage showing front and rear perspectives of one vehicle, one dominant view and
one narrow perspective rail; then compact editorial rows for included work,
3-5-day duration, 5-year warranty and taxi. Premium automotive magazine rhythm,
large photographic planes, precise rules, no card grid. Keep controls and text
areas clearly implementable in HTML. No long readable copy, no gradients, no
glassmorphism, no diagrams, no swatch configurator, no fake reviews, no fake
logos or plate text, no workers or hands, no mountains, no toy car, no broken
anatomy. Internal CONCEPT_TARGET only.
