# Wave48 campaign landing contract

- Experiment: `wave48_ya_search_color_film_230k_control`.
- Candidate destination: `https://go.detailingspb.ru/color-film/`.
- Audience: Saint Petersburg and nearby areas; commercial intent around full color-film wrapping, body color change and a concrete installed price.
- Offer: full wrapping of painted body elements with color protective film for a fixed `230 000 RUB`.
- Included on-page: material, body preparation, installation and final control.
- Duration: `3-5 calendar days`.
- Warranty: `5 years` for material and installation; detailed conditions are confirmed by the administrator.
- Consultation: free detailing body wash, without a subsequent-booking condition in public copy.
- Full-wrap service: taxi to the customer's destination after vehicle intake and back for handover.
- Primary conversion: meaningful Telegram, WhatsApp, MAX or phone contact about color-film wrapping.
- Qualified lead: vehicle plus color-film request plus an agreed next step.
- Soft signals: price/proof/process/warranty views, gallery use, scroll, reviews and route clicks.
- Legal note: page information is not a public offer; the administrator confirms exact scope and conditions before work.
- Control urgency: no timer. Deadline and limited-slot hypotheses require separate URLs, UTMs and cohorts.
- Test boundary proposal: 60 comparable clean visits, `3 000 RUB`, or 14 calendar days. Early stop only for broken tracking/contact path, uncontrolled spend or more than 30 percent irrelevant spend.
