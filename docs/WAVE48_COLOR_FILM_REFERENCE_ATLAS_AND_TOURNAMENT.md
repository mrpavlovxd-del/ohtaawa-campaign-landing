## Selected direction

The page opens as a premium automotive editorial rather than a detailing price
list. A controlled atmospheric hero establishes desire; a real two-perspective
case immediately restores factual trust. Cool ivory creates reading space,
near-black controls contrast, restrained lime keeps OHTAAWA identity, and a
deep cool burgundy rule connects the interface to the actual wrapped vehicle.

## Structural divergence check

- Hero composition is split by editorial rhythm, not the Wave45 centered dark
  offer stack.
- The signature front/rear stage replaces card grids and diagram blocks.
- Proof is integrated near the top instead of being deferred to a generic
  gallery.
- Burgundy is a material cue, not a page-wide monochrome theme.
- No fixed mobile CTA bar; the contact action appears at natural decision
points and in the final contact sheet.
