# Wave46 design direction

## Thesis

The page should feel like a precise protective decision for the front of a new car: less commitment than a full-body wrap, but the same calm studio discipline and finish quality.

## Visual metaphor

`Front line`: one close, cinematic three-quarter view of the vehicle front, followed by large photographic details of the surfaces that receive the most road impact. No drawn car diagrams or floating schematic shapes.

## First viewport

- OHTAAWA brand lockup and a compact navigation.
- Full-bleed front-quarter studio image.
- Literal headline: `Защита зон риска прозрачной пленкой`.
- Fixed price: `60 000 RUB`.
- One primary consultation CTA plus a quiet link to package contents.
- Free detailing wash during consultation as a concise trust cue.
- No timer in the control version.

## Page flow

1. Offer and price.
2. What the package protects, expressed as editorial text next to real/approved close-up media.
3. Why these surfaces receive the first damage.
4. Proof carousel with approved OHTAAWA media.
5. Material, installation, and warranty.
6. Process, reviews, location, FAQ, final CTA.

## Material and type

- Keep Wave45 black/ivory/lime brand language and serif/sans hierarchy.
- Use fewer sections and stronger full-width photography.
- Avoid cards-as-layout, tables, fake technical infographics, gradients, and decorative blobs.
- Buttons and dialogs stay code-native and accessible.
- Mobile is recomposed without a fixed bottom CTA.
