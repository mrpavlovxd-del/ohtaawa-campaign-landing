# Wave46 campaign landing contract

- Experiment: `wave46_ya_search_risk_zones_60k_control`.
- Destination: `https://go.detailingspb.ru/risk-zones/`.
- Audience: SPb and nearby areas; users searching for protection of the front/risk zones, a new-car film package, or a concrete price.
- Offer: protection of the risk zones with transparent polyurethane film for a fixed `60 000 RUB`.
- Package language: bumper, hood, and the most vulnerable painted elements of the front; the administrator confirms the exact composition before work begins.
- Primary conversion: meaningful messenger dialog or call about this package.
- Qualified lead: vehicle + request + agreed next step.
- Soft signals: price/package/proof views, carousel interactions, scroll, reviews and map clicks.
- Consultation: free detailing body wash during the consultation; no subsequent-booking condition on the page.
- Warranty: five years for film and installation; detailed terms are confirmed by the administrator.
- Legal note: page information is not a public offer.
- Control urgency: none. Deadline/timer hypotheses must use separate URLs/UTMs and must not mutate this control.
- Test boundary: 60 comparable clean visits, 3,000 RUB, or 14 calendar days unless tracking breaks, spend becomes uncontrolled, or irrelevant spend exceeds 30 percent.
