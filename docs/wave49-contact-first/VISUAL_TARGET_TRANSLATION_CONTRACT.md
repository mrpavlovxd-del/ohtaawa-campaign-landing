# Wave49 Visual Target Translation Contract

## Target

The existing Wave45 production landing is the binding visual target. Wave49 must look like the same OHTAAWA campaign object, not a new page or an attached third-party contact widget.

## Elements that must remain unchanged

- hero photograph, crop logic, typography and price hierarchy;
- trust rail and all following sections;
- proof carousel, location block, guarantee and FAQ;
- dark graphite surface, restrained green accent, square geometry and tight radii.

## Contact-first translation

- Place one compact dark contact console inside the hero copy column.
- Lead with «Узнать ближайшее окно», followed by one sentence explaining the response.
- Present Telegram, WhatsApp, MAX and phone as equal code-native actions.
- Use the existing line, color, typography and icon-circle language.
- On mobile, recompose into a stable 2x2 action grid; never create a sticky bottom bar.

## Translation loss limits

- No visible mismatch in color, radius, typography or spacing relative to Wave45.
- The contact console must not push the price out of the first mobile viewport.
- The next-section hint must remain visible or immediately reachable.
- All text, focus, hit areas and tracking stay code-native and accessible.
